02 / PROJECTS

Things I've built.

A selection of personal projects — from custom mechanical designs to games and software applications.

01 IN USE

NFC Smart Desk Phone Holder

A custom-designed smartphone holder combining Fusion 360, 3D printing and NFC-based automation. Placing the phone on the holder activates a custom workspace setup: desk lighting is enabled, other room lights are switched off and Do Not Disturb is activated.

Fusion 360 3D Printing NFC Automation
02 IN USE

Custom USB Hub & Desk Organizer

A precision-fit desktop system designed around three USB hubs. The design integrates storage for USB drives, TF and SD cards and a pen holder while remaining securely attached without screws, magnets or external mounting hardware.

Fusion 360 CAD 3D Printing Mechanical Design
03 PROJECT

GraviWorld

A 2D platformer built around a gravity-based gameplay mechanic. Special gravity plates alter the player's gravitational behavior in different ways, creating movement challenges and opportunities for level design.

C# Unity Android Game Development
04 IN USE

Modular Magnetic Keyboard Stand

A modular keyboard mounting system featuring a magnetic base, detachable wrist rest and a 22.5° angled support. The components are designed to connect magnetically and can be rearranged without tools.

Fusion 360 CAD 3D Printing Mechanical Design
05 PROJECT

CatGame

A 2D top-down action game for Android in which players control a cat with different abilities while fighting mice and dogs. Interactive food bowls restore health and add an additional gameplay mechanic beyond combat.

C# Unity Android Game Development
06 PROJECT

Modular Card Holder

A compact card holder designed in Fusion 360 around interchangeable front plates. The modular design allows the same base to support different use cases and visual configurations.

Fusion 360 CAD 3D Printing Modular Design
